Refund Policy

Last Updated: July 10, 2026

We want you to love your pieces as much as we loved creating them. If your purchase isn't a perfect fit, we are here to help you coordinate a seamless return or exchange.

1. Return Window

We accept return requests within 10 days of your delivery date. Items sent back to us without first requesting a return will not be accepted.

2. Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for a full refund, your garments must meet the following strict conditions:

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, and undamaged.
  • All original tags, packaging, and designer labels must be securely attached.
  • Free from alterations, cosmetics, perfume, or deodorant stains.

3. Non-Returnable Items

The following items are considered final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged:

  • Custom-made, bespoke, or altered garments.
  • Intimates/underwears, swimwear, and earrings (for hygiene reasons).
  • Items purchased during final warehouse clearance sales.

4. The Process

Follow these steps to complete your return:

  1. Visit our online Returns Portal or email dripsupport@dripstar.com with your order number.
  2. Once approved, we will email you a prepaid shipping label and instructions.
  3. Pack your items securely and drop the package off at your nearest designated courier.

5. Processing & Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your item, we will notify you of the approval status. Approved refunds are processed automatically back to your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Please note that banks or credit card providers may take additional time to post the transaction.

6. Exchanges

The fastest way to get a different size or color is to return your original item. Once your return is accepted, you can make a separate purchase for the new piece.

7. Contact Our Concierge

If you have any questions regarding your shipment, sizing concerns, or a damaged delivery, please contact us immediately:

Email: dripsupport@dripstar.com
Hours: Monday – Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM (EST)

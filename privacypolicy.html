Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 10, 2026

At DRIPSTAR ORIGINALS, we value your trust and respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share your personal information when you visit, make a purchase, or interact with our online boutique.

1. Information We Collect

When you shop with us, we collect information necessary to fulfill your order and enhance your styling experience. This includes:

  • Contact Details: Your name, shipping address, billing address, phone number, and email address.
  • Payment Information: Credit card details or alternative payment methods (processed securely via our payment gateways).
  • Device & Usage Data: Your IP address, browser type, and how you browse our collections, collected automatically via cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data to curate the best fashion experience possible, including:

  • Processing, shipping, and tracking your orders.
  • Managing your lookbook, account, and purchase history.
  • Sending tailored style updates, trend reports, and exclusive offers (you can opt out at any time).
  • Improving our website layout and customer service.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We only share your information with trusted third-party partners who help us run our business, such as:

  • Courier Services: To deliver your curated garments safely to your doorstep.
  • Payment Processors: To handle secure billing transactions.
  • Marketing Tools: To send you relevant news and seasonal discounts if you have subscribed.

4. Your Rights & Choices

You have full control over your data. Depending on your location, you have the right to request access to the data we hold, correct inaccuracies, or ask for the deletion of your personal details. You can also disable cookies through your browser settings.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please reach out to our dedicated concierge team:

Email: dripsupport@dripstar.com
